Warning Signs to Watch For

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you money and keep a slow drip from turning into a flooded utility room. If any of the following sound familiar, it's worth having the unit checked:

  • Loud rumbling or popping that flushing no longer fixes
  • Water seeping out from under the tank jacket
  • A water heater that is more than about ten years old
  • Hot water running out faster every month
  • Rusty or brown water from the hot side only
  • Rust flaking from the tank jacket or its connections
  • Energy bills rising while hot water gets harder to come by
  • Repairs that keep coming back, one part after another

Water Heater Care Tips From Our Technicians

Routine maintenance is almost always cheaper and easier than an early replacement. Here's where to begin:

  • Avoid running the tank hotter than about 120 degrees unless there is a specific need
  • Keep household water pressure in a safe range to protect the tank and its fittings
  • Note the install date and keep up with maintenance so the next replacement is never an emergency
  • Flush the new tank once or twice a year to keep sediment from building up
  • Heat pump units need a clean filter and open airflow to run efficiently
  • A fresh anode rod at the right time is the cheapest way to extend tank life
  • If you chose tankless, flush it annually to clear scale from the heat exchanger
