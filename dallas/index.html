How Dallas Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a water heater that lasts and one that floods the garage in year seven. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a yearly flush, an anode rod check every three to five years, a yearly test of the T&P valve, a thermostat set near 120 degrees, and annual descaling for tankless units on hard water, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

How old is your water heater? The rating plate on the tank usually encodes the month and year it was built, and that date says a lot about what comes next. Most tank heaters reach 8 to 12 years, heat pump units 10 to 15, and well-maintained tankless units about 20. If your heater is past that range and also shows rusty water, popping sounds, or dampness under the tank, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement after a leak.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Consider a spent anode rod: replacing it is a quick, inexpensive repair, but if nobody replaces it, the water begins corroding the tank steel, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a surprise cleanup.

Typical Water Heater Issues in Dallas

Whatever type of water heater your Dallas home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tank heaters tend to have trouble with thermocouples and flame sensors, dirty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric tanks burn out heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless units show error codes for scale buildup, ignition faults, and flow sensor trouble, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
